02.1 — Featured project

Career Assessment
and Placement System

Lead Developer / Systems Developer DILG Region III 2025 — Present Team of 9
caps.region3.dilg.gov.ph

Designed as the first DILG Region III system of its kind — a dedicated applicant portal and a dedicated HR portal alongside an internal organizational system, aligned with the office's QMS process.

The problem

Recruitment, selection and placement ran on manual workflows and physical paper. Every applicant submitted hard copies of the same documentary requirements — PDS, eligibility, diploma, work experience, training certificates — and every one of those had to be received, filed, retrieved and circulated by hand. Applicant information was not centralized, and evaluation records lived apart from the documents they were based on.

The objective

Digitalize the recruitment process end to end: let applicants apply and submit requirements as soft copies, give HR a single place to manage, review, evaluate and score those applicants, and keep the whole thing aligned with the office's existing QMS process rather than working around it.

The solution

Three connected surfaces on one centralized data layer — an applicant portal, an HR portal, and an internal system for organizational users — with role-based access control deciding what each type of user can see and do. Documentary requirements are uploaded once and accessed from anywhere they are needed, including by provincial directors.

System architecture

Applicant Portal

The public-facing surface. Applicants create an application against a posted vacancy, upload their documentary requirements as soft copies, and follow the status of that application without having to call or visit the office.

Scale 1,300+ applicants have gone through this portal, each submitting a minimum of five documentary requirements as soft copies.
  • 01ApplicationApply against posted vacancies directly through the portal.
  • 02Document submissionUpload required documents as soft copies — a minimum of five per application.
  • 03Application trackingFollow the progress of a submitted application.
  • 04Applicant informationMaintain personal and employment details in one record.

HR Portal

Where the recruitment work actually happens. HR personnel work through applicants per vacancy, review the submitted documents against requirements, and record evaluation and scoring inside the same system that holds the evidence.

Why it matters Evaluation records and the documents they are based on now live in the same place, instead of in separate paper trails.
  • 01Applicant managementFilter, search and act on applicants per vacancy and appointment type.
  • 02Document reviewView and download submitted requirements against a completeness check.
  • 03EvaluationStructured assessment across exams, interview and comparative assessment.
  • 04ScoringRatings from multiple raters tallied into a final average and ranking.
  • 05Recruitment workflowApplication status moved through the office's selection process.

Internal User System

The administrative layer beneath both portals. It defines who exists in the system, what role they hold, and which permissions that role carries — down to per-user allow and deny overrides on individual capabilities.

Access model Role-based access by default, with per-user exceptions handled as explicit inherit, allow or deny overrides rather than one-off accounts.
  • 01User managementCreate and maintain internal accounts across the organization.
  • 02Role-based accessRoles such as HR Section, Regional Director and Unit/Section Head, each with its own access.
  • 03Permission overridesInherit, allow or deny individual permissions per user as an exception.
  • 04Administrative functionsVacancy posting, activity logs and backup management.
  • 05Centralized dataOne source of applicant records, documents and evaluations.

My role

Lead developer and team lead. I personally built the frontend, backend and UI/UX, set up hosting from development through production, and established the team's Git workflow — while coordinating a nine-person team of QA, UI, frontend and backend members as its youngest and newest member.

  • Requirements gathering with HR and organizational stakeholders
  • System planning and workflow design around the existing QMS process
  • System architecture and relational data design
  • Frontend, backend and UI/UX development
  • Hosting setup and deployment, development through production
  • Git workflow and team coordination
  • Testing, implementation and continuous improvement

Impact

  • Digitized a recruitment process that previously ran on manual workflows and physical paper
  • Centralized applicant information, documents and evaluation records in one system
  • Gave applicants and HR personnel dedicated portals for the first time in the regional office
  • Made documentary requirements accessible to the people who need them, including provincial directors

03.1 — Undergraduate thesis

SignSaya

Bridging Communication Gaps for Deaf Filipino Travelers using a Filipino Sign Language Interpretation System. Smart gloves and a companion mobile application that interpret Filipino Sign Language gestures as common travel questions, built to help solo Deaf travellers navigate unfamiliar places through two-way communication.

Second Lead Developer Group thesis February — March 2024 IoT · Embedded · Mobile

My role

SignSaya was a group thesis. I served as second lead developer — personally handling the ESP32-to-Flutter application connection over Bluetooth Low Energy, contributing to the written manuscript, and coordinating with the external partner group DOST-EPDC.

How it works

An ESP32 processes readings from E.C.V. finger sensors (electric tape, conductive fabric and Velostat) together with an ICM-20948. Data processed by the Digital Motion Processor is fed to an AI model, transmitted over Bluetooth Low Energy and received by an Android application capable of translating into a specific language.

Method & result

Built using the iterative waterfall model.

68.57%

Interpreted accuracy, validated by Engr. Abiel B. Bulanadi, ECT. Evaluation results indicate the system is Functional, Reliable, Usable and Very Efficient.

User validation

User experience feedback from the Deaf Association of the City of San Fernando, Pampanga rated the system Very Satisfied on ease of use, user interface, performance and overall satisfaction.

Stack

  • ESP32 · ICM-20948 · E.C.V. finger sensors
  • Bluetooth Low Energy
  • Flutter (Android application)
  • SQLite local storage
  • Speech-to-text and language translation

Why it matters here

It is the clearest evidence of the Computer Engineering foundation: embedded systems, IoT, mobile development and accessibility treated as a single engineering problem rather than separate disciplines.
